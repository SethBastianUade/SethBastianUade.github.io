About

I am a product designer focused on creating disciplined digital experiences that prioritize clarity over decoration. My approach is rooted in archival research and functional minimalism, ensuring that every interface element serves a deliberate structural purpose.

With over eight years of experience working across fintech and healthcare sectors, I specialize in translating complex systemic requirements into intuitive, editorial-grade user interfaces. I believe that good design should be felt through its ease of use rather than seen through unnecessary flourishes.
